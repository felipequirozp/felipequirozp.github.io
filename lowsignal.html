01 / Company

Building a company, not just campaigns.

lowsignal is built around a simple idea: companies do not need more disconnected marketing activity — they need systems that connect strategy, execution, technology and growth.

The goal is to create an operating layer that helps companies move faster, communicate better and build repeatable ways to grow.

02 / My role

Operating the system behind the company.

As Co-Founder & COO, my role sits across operations, product, team coordination, business development and the systems that allow lowsignal to deliver consistently.

  • Company operations and internal systems.
  • Product and service development.
  • Team structure and execution.

  • New products, partnerships and business opportunities.
