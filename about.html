02 / About

From ideas to real operating systems.

I’m Felipe Quiroz, a product and growth operator based in Santiago, Chile. My work has moved across product management, company building and partnerships — but the common thread is turning ideas into systems that people actually use.

Today, I work on workforce management products at GeoVictoria and build lowsignal, a growth company focused on connecting strategy, technology and execution.

Before that, I worked across partnerships and education technology at TetherEd, connecting products with institutions, public systems and new markets.

I’m particularly interested in products, emerging technology, new business models and the systems behind how companies operate and grow.
