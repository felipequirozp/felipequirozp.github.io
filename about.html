About

I turn ambiguity into products, systems and growth.

I’m Felipe Quiroz, a Product Manager and Co-Founder based in Santiago, Chile. I work at the intersection of product, growth, operations and business, usually in environments where the problem is still taking shape and execution matters as much as strategy. At GeoVictoria, I lead the evolution of Workforce Management products within a SaaS ecosystem serving 10,000+ customers across 20+ countries. At lowsignal, I lead operations, commercial strategy and delivery while building the systems the company needs to scale.

Before that, I led partnerships, business development and market expansion at TetherEd across Latin America, the U.S. and Europe, connecting technology with public and private education systems. Across those roles, I’ve learned to move comfortably between strategy and execution: understanding the problem, aligning teams and stakeholders, building the operating model and pushing the work until it creates measurable value.
