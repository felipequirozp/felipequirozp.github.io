02 / About

From opportunities to systems that actually work.

I’m Felipe Quiroz, a Product Manager and Co-Founder based in Santiago, Chile. My experience spans product, growth, partnerships and company building, with one common thread: turning problems and opportunities into systems that can operate, learn and scale. Today, I lead Workforce Management products at GeoVictoria and build lowsignal, where I work across operations, commercial strategy, product and Growth Systems.

Before that, at TetherEd, I worked across partnerships, expansion and education technology, connecting product with institutions, public systems and new markets. That journey strengthened my interest in applied AI, products that live close to real operations, and new ways of building companies with small teams, technology and well-designed systems.
