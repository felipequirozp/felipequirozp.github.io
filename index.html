How I think

A few things
I keep coming back to.

Not rules, more like notes to self. Ideas I’ve picked up in classrooms, sales calls and product reviews that still hold up.

  1. 01

    Listen longer than feels necessary.

    The real problem is usually one question deeper than the first answer.

  2. 02

    Good ideas come from other fields.

    Some of my best ideas came from teaching, books or conversations far from the topic at hand.

  3. 03

    Stay a beginner somewhere.

    Always be learning something you’re bad at. It keeps you humble and curious.
