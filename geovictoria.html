The focus is not only on defining features, but on understanding whether a product actually solves a meaningful operational problem.

04 / Context

Workforce management is an operating system.

Scheduling, optimization and shift changes may look like isolated workflows, but they sit inside a much larger operational system involving people, regulations, demand, staffing and business constraints.

That complexity is what makes the category interesting: good product decisions have to work for the business, the operator and the people actually using the system.
