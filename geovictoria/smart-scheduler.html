THE CHALLENGE

A schedule is only useful if it works in real life.

Building a calendar is simple. Building one that respects the operation is something else.

Planners have to manage rotating patterns, weekly-hour limits, consecutive working days and rest periods while adapting to changes in staffing and coverage.

The product challenge was to make planning feel flexible and immediate without hiding the rules that determine whether a schedule is valid.

PRODUCT CAPABILITIES / 01 TO 03

Build it. Check it.
Make it repeatable.

Three connected capabilities make complex rotating schedules easier to create and manage in one place.

01 / CALENDAR

Plan the whole rotation.

Work across weekly and monthly views, with direct editing and drag-and-drop changes to shifts.

02 / ALERTS

See problems before publishing.

Surface warnings around hours, consecutive days and other scheduling inconsistencies while building the plan.

03 / TEMPLATES

Reuse proven patterns.

Apply recurring schemes, including examples like 4×4, 7×7 and 5×2, to individuals or groups of employees.

THE PLANNING FLOW / 01 TO 04

From a blank calendar to a publishable schedule.

The planner brings building, validation and publication into one workflow. The goal is to spot issues while the schedule can still be changed.

THE ACTUAL PRODUCT

DESIGNED WITH OPERATIONS

Better planning starts with better feedback.

Customer discovery and validation sessions helped identify where planners encountered confusion, which rules needed immediate feedback and how the experience could remain flexible without sacrificing compliance.

That input fed into product priorities and refinements with Product and Engineering, bringing the daily reality of workforce operations into the scheduling experience.
