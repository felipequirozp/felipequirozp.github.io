Problem

Employee flexibility creates operational complexity fast.

Changing a shift sounds simple until the product needs to respect staffing coverage, rest requirements, approval logic, monthly limits and the catalog of valid shifts at the same time.

The challenge was to create an experience employees could understand while preserving the controls operations teams needed to keep schedules valid and predictable.

Rules & guardrails

The workflow had to make flexibility safe by design.

01 / Rest

12-hour minimum rest.

Changes had to preserve the required rest window between shifts.

02 / Approval

48-hour auto-approval.

Approval behavior was designed around timing rules and operational control.

03 / Limits

One exchange per month.

Monthly limits helped prevent uncontrolled changes and edge-case overload.

04 / Catalog

Valid shifts only.

Changes were constrained to the available shift catalog and scheduling rules.

Workflow logic

A simple front-end flow backed by operational rules.

01
RequestAn employee starts a shift change from the available options.

ValidateThe product checks rest, catalog, limits and other operational constraints.

04
UpdateThe accepted change becomes part of the planning flow without breaking schedule consistency.
