Problem

Shift planning is a constraint problem, not a calendar problem.

Operations teams need to balance demand, employee availability, labor rules, coverage requirements and real-world exceptions at the same time. A planner that ignores any of those layers quickly creates conflicts, manual corrections or staffing gaps.

The product challenge was to turn that complexity into a decision-support experience that felt understandable to planners and robust enough for enterprise operations.

Product logic

Three inputs shaped the optimization model.

01 / Demand

What the operation needs.

Coverage and staffing requirements by day, shift and operational context.

02 / Availability

Who can actually work.

Employee availability, absences and practical constraints around assignments.

03 / Constraints

What cannot be broken.

Operational rules, rest requirements and other conditions that make a schedule valid.
