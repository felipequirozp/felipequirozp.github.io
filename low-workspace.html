Low WorkspaceRun the company.

I designed and built Low Workspace, an internal operating platform for lowsignal that brings client management, revenue visibility, tasks and team operations into one place.

ProductOperationsAI-assisted developmentInternal tools
The challenge

Growth created more tools, more handoffs and less visibility.

As lowsignal grew, commercial information, delivery, client context and financial tracking began to live across different tools and workflows. The problem was not a lack of software. It was fragmentation.

I wanted a single operating layer that matched how we actually run the company: simple enough for the team to use every day, structured enough to give us a clear view of the business.

The product

One workspace for the operating system behind lowsignal.

Low Workspace centralizes the information we need to make decisions and execute: accounts, revenue, recurring revenue, tasks and operational visibility. Instead of adapting our company to a generic tool, I built the workflows around the way lowsignal works.

01Clients

02Revenue & MRR

Product principle

Internal tools should reduce operational noise, not create more of it.

Low Workspace is a practical expression of how I think about product and operations: start from a real workflow, remove friction, make the important information visible, and keep iterating with the people who use it.
