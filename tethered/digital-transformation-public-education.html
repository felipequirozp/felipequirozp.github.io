The problem

Public enrollment was not one process. It was dozens of workflows stitched together.

School networks needed to coordinate families, administrators, documents, placement and enrollment across many institutions. The challenge was not simply putting forms online; it was creating infrastructure that could absorb operational complexity without making the experience harder for schools or families.

01 / Fragmentation

Many institutions, many workflows.

Different schools, teams and operating realities had to work inside one consistent process.

02 / Visibility

Limited operational visibility.

Manual steps made status tracking, follow-up and coordination harder across the network.

03 / Scale

High-volume family interactions.

Any digital solution had to remain usable while handling thousands of real enrollment journeys.
