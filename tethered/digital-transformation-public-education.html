The challenge

Digitizing a form is easy.
Connecting a system isn’t.

Enrollment is more than one step. It’s a journey involving families, schools and public administration.

Large networks had to coordinate documents, placement, follow-up and enrollment across institutions with very different realities, without adding complexity for the schools and families using them.

The enrollment journey

From disconnected steps
to one clearer process.

A digital flow connecting each stage of enrollment. Four practical moments, one shared process.

01 · Intake

Start with the family.

Capture student and family information through a structured digital application.

02 · Documents

Organize what schools need.

Collect and validate the documents and information each enrollment requires.

03 · Coordination

Keep institutions in sync.

Give administrative teams clear visibility into progress, pending steps and follow-up.

04 · Enrollment

Complete it digitally.

From first intake to confirmed enrollment, with a more consistent experience for everyone.
