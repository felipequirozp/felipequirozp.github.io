Making school waitlists accessible nationwide.

Anótate en la Lista helped move Chile's school vacancy regularization process from physical queues to a shared online registry, connecting families looking for seats with schools managing available places.

THE CHALLENGE

Access to a school seat shouldn't depend on standing in a line.

For families looking for available school places, regularization had often meant visiting schools in person.

Vacancy information, waiting lists and follow-up were managed through processes that could differ between institutions, making an already stressful moment harder to navigate.

The original 2023–24 implementation focused on a common digital registry, helping families express interest remotely and schools manage requests through a more consistent process.

THE SERVICE JOURNEY / 01 TO 03

From physical queues to one digital process.

Three essential moments in the original regularization workflow, connecting the family experience with school operations.

01 / REGISTER
01 / REGISTER

Express interest remotely.

Families could register requests for seats at public and subsidized private schools without visiting each institution in person.

02 / ORGANIZE

Bring requests into one workflow.

Schools could manage applications and waiting lists through a shared digital process, making requests easier to follow.

03 / RESPOND

Connect vacancies with demand.

Available places could be addressed in line with registration order and the admissions rules applicable to the process.
