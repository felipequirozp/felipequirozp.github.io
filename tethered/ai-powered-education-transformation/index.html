THE CHALLENGE

AI adoption is a people and systems challenge, not just a tool rollout.

Providing access to generative AI is only the beginning. Making it useful in public schools takes a different kind of work.

Schools have to consider pedagogy, staff readiness, equity and the realities of day-to-day operations.

Responsible implementation also means building trust and giving teachers and school teams practical ways to understand both the opportunities and the limits of the technology.

ADOPTION JOURNEY / 01 TO 03

From access to responsible adoption.

Three connected priorities for exploring generative AI with school communities, without assuming ideal conditions.
