Bringing responsible generative AI into public education.

An international initiative developed with Columbia University, OpenAI, Tether Education and SLEP del Pino to explore how generative AI could be adopted effectively, responsibly and equitably in Chilean public schools.

The challenge

AI adoption in schools is an operating problem, not a tool rollout.

Introducing generative AI into public education required more than access to technology. Schools needed a model that considered pedagogy, implementation, equity, staff readiness and responsible use inside real institutional constraints.

01 / Access

Technology had to be usable.

Tools needed to fit the realities of public schools, staff and students rather than assume ideal conditions.

02 / Adoption

Communities needed context.

Teachers and school teams needed practical ways to understand where AI could help and where caution was required.

03 / Responsibility

Governance mattered from day one.

Responsible use, equity and institutional trust had to be part of implementation rather than an afterthought.

In the field

Implementation happened with school communities, not around them.

The program was designed around real use and feedback. That meant working with schools to understand where generative AI could create value, where friction appeared and what support was needed for responsible adoption.

Outcome

A practical model for exploring AI inside real education systems.

The strongest result was not a single feature or classroom demo. It was creating the conditions for institutions to test, learn and build capability around generative AI while keeping responsibility, equity and operational reality in the loop.
