AI-powered Education Transformation.

An international initiative developed with Columbia University, OpenAI, Tether Education and SLEP del Pino to accelerate responsible and equitable generative AI adoption in Chilean public schools.

02 / My role

Connecting research, institutions and implementation.

I led implementation across participating schools, coordinating execution, stakeholders and the connection between research, institutional needs and AI-enabled solutions.

The work required moving across different layers: understanding each education community's context, coordinating public actors and internal teams, and turning research objectives into experiences that could work in real schools.
