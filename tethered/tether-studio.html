THE CHALLENGE

School photography had too many disconnected moving parts.

A photograph is simple. The operation behind it rarely is.

Portraits, events, yearbooks, parental consent, family distribution and administration often lived across vendors, forms, files and manual follow-up.

The opportunity was to connect those stages into one visible product experience, making the process easier for school teams to manage and simpler for families to use.

ONE CONNECTED WORKFLOW / 01 TO 05

Five stages.
One continuous flow.

From the moment a photograph is taken to the way schools and families access it, the value comes from joining the entire journey.

01 / CAPTURE
01 / CAPTURE

Bring photography into one place.

School portraits and event photography enter the same digital workflow rather than being managed through scattered handoffs.

02 / CONSENT

Make permissions traceable.

Link parental consent to the content it governs, so school teams can manage permissions with greater visibility.

03 / ORGANIZE

Connect content with context.

Organize portraits, events and yearbook material by school and student, reducing manual sorting and follow-up.

04 / FAMILY ACCESS

Simplify the family experience.

Give families a clearer way to access the photography relevant to them without navigating multiple channels.

05 / ADMINISTRATION

Make the operation easier to run.

Provide school teams with one more consistent process for monitoring content, access and remaining tasks.

BUILDING IN THE FIELD

Designed for real school operations.

The product was shaped around the coordination involved in school photography, with a focus on traceable consent, simpler access and less administrative friction.

THE OUTCOME

Less coordination.
More room for what matters.

By connecting what had been separate steps, the product aimed to reduce manual overhead across the photography journey while keeping permissions and access visible.
