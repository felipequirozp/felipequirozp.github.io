The problem

School choice is hard when information is scattered, inconsistent or difficult to compare.

Families often need to make high-impact education decisions using fragmented sources, institutional websites and incomplete context. The product challenge was to make discovery useful without overwhelming users.

01 / Discovery

Too much information, not enough clarity.

Families needed a simpler way to understand relevant options around them.

02 / Comparison

Schools were difficult to compare.

Different formats and sources made side-by-side evaluation harder than it should be.

03 / Guidance

Context mattered more than listings.

The experience needed to help users interpret information, not just display it.

The product

One discovery layer across search, comparison and guidance.

The Explorer combined product, data, AI and partnerships to make educational information clearer and more useful for families and communities.

01Map-based searchDiscover schools by geography and surrounding context.
02Personalized filtersNarrow options based on needs, preferences and available information.
03Digital school profilesBring key institutional information into a more consistent structure.
04AI guidanceHelp families interpret options and navigate complex decisions.
