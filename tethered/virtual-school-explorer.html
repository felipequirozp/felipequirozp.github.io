THE CHALLENGE

Choosing a school shouldn't mean piecing together scattered information.

School choice is a big decision. Finding useful, comparable information is often the hard part.

Families navigate institutional websites, inconsistent data and incomplete context to understand what options may be right for them.

The product challenge was to make discovery clearer and more useful, helping families interpret information rather than simply listing schools.
