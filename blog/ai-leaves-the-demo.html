What AI looks like when it leaves the demo

AI Week Chile focused on something more useful than the promise of technology: how AI is already changing growth, efficiency and execution.

Felipe Quiroz Product, Growth & Operations
August 2026 · Santiago, Chile
AI Week Chile · Santiago, Chile
In this article
01 / Beyond the promise 02 / AI applied to real growth 03 / From use case to workflow 04 / What I took away

AI Week Chile brought companies together to share concrete experiences implementing AI. That made the conversation much more useful: fewer predictions and more questions about what is actually working today.

01 / Beyond the promise

The interesting discussion is no longer just about what a model can do. It is about what changes when a company embeds that capability into a real process: growth, service, operations or product.

The cases that interest me most are not necessarily the flashiest ones. They are the ones that reduce friction, speed up decisions or increase a team’s capacity without replacing human judgment.

02 / AI applied to real growth

One thing about AI Week made a lot of sense to me: connecting AI with growth, efficiency and organizational transformation. That forces the conversation toward impact rather than novelty.

The useful question

Instead of asking “where can we add AI?”, start with “what real constraint are we trying to remove?”.

03 / From use case to workflow

A demo can live in isolation. A workflow has to coexist with data, permissions, exceptions, users and existing processes. That is where the real product work begins.

AI starts creating leverage when it stops being the center of the experience and becomes a layer within the system.

04 / What I took away

The most valuable part of the week was comparing how different teams are solving similar problems and learning from real experience.

The interesting part of AI is no longer what it can do. It’s what companies are learning to do with it.
