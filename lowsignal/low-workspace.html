Low WorkspaceRun the company.

I designed and built Low Workspace, an internal operating platform for lowsignal that brings client management, revenue visibility, tasks and team operations into one place.

Product, Design & Developmentlowsignal · Internal PlatformSantiago · Chile
The challenge

More growth created more tools, more handoffs and less visibility.

As lowsignal grew, commercial information, delivery, client context and financial tracking started living across different tools and workflows. The problem was not a lack of software. It was fragmentation.

I wanted one operating layer that matched how we actually run the company: simple enough to use every day, structured enough to give us a clear view of the business.

The product

One workspace for the operating system behind lowsignal.

Low Workspace centralizes the information we need to make decisions and execute: accounts, monthly revenue, recurring revenue, tasks and operational visibility. Instead of adapting our company to a generic tool, I built the workflows around the way lowsignal works.

01Clients

Centralized account information and commercial context.

02Revenue & MRR

Monthly revenue visibility in CLP and recurring client tracking.

03Tasks

A shared layer for priorities, ownership and execution.

04Operations

Key workflows and company information in one place.

05Team visibility

A clearer picture of what is moving, blocked or needs attention.

06Built to evolve

An internal product that can grow alongside the company.

What I built

From operating problemto working product.

I owned the product end to end: defining the problem, structuring the information architecture, designing the workflows and interface, and building the application with AI-assisted development tools.

01Product definition

Translated internal operating needs into a focused product scope.

02UX & information architecture

Designed navigation, hierarchy and workflows around daily use.

03AI-assisted development

Used modern AI coding workflows to move from concept to working software faster.

04Iteration

Continuously evolving the product based on real internal usage.

Inside the product

Designed for the team.
Built for the work.

The interface carries lowsignal’s visual language into an internal product built for fast scanning, operational clarity and everyday use.

Product experience

The brand starts before the dashboard.

The login experience keeps the product visually connected to lowsignal, using the company’s “Less noise. More growth.” language without adding unnecessary interface noise.

Product principle

Internal tools should reduce operational noise, not create more of it.

Low Workspace is a practical expression of how I think about product and operations: start from a real workflow, remove friction, make the important information visible, and keep iterating with the people who use it.
