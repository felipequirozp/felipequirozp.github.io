01 / Role

Partnerships around a complex product problem.

TetherEd builds user-centered guidance products for school choice and admissions — helping families navigate complex education systems and make better-informed decisions.

As Head of Partnerships, my role was to connect that product with education systems, public institutions and partners across different markets, turning institutional relationships into real deployments and adoption.

02 / Scale

Building reach across education.

The platform connected education options, families and institutions across Latin America, the United States, Denmark and Spain — combining school discovery, admissions guidance and institutional partnerships.
