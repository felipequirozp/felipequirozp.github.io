What Tether built

A platform connecting school choice, admissions and enrollment.

Tether operated across B2G, B2B and B2C models, working with public education systems, school networks, institutions and families. The common thread was digital infrastructure for complex education decisions.

01 / B2G

Public education.

Digital enrollment, assignment and discovery infrastructure for public systems and school networks.

02 / B2B

Institutions & networks.

Tools and workflows that help schools manage admissions, enrollment and family engagement.

03 / B2C

Families.

Discovery and decision-making experiences that make school choice easier to navigate.
